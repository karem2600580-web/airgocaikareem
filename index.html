Policies

Payment is held until delivery

The sender pays when the trip is booked. Airgo holds the money and releases it to the traveler once the sender confirms with the delivery code. Our fee is taken at that moment.

Banned items
  • Drugs, weapons, and anything illegal in either country
  • Cash, jewelry, and valuables over 200 USD
  • Medicine without a doctor’s note
  • Batteries, liquids, and perishable food on long trips
  • Sealed packages the traveler cannot open and check
Traveler checks before carrying

Travelers must see the item and may open the package at hand-over. A traveler can refuse any item, and no one can pressure them to carry something they are unsure about.

Cancellations and refunds

If a traveler cancels or the flight changes, the sender gets a full refund. If a sender cancels more than 48 hours before the flight, the refund is full minus a small processing cost. After that, the traveler keeps 50%.

Lost or damaged items

Report within 48 hours of the delivery date. We review messages, photos, and hand-over records. Refunds cover the carrying fee, and item value is covered up to 100 USD for verified users.

Verified users

Everyone verifies their phone and a government ID before their first trip or request. Travelers also add their flight booking, which we check against the trip date.

Customs and local law

Travelers are responsible for customs rules on arrival. Senders must declare accurate contents and value.
